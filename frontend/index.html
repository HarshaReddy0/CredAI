How it works

Four steps, one conversation

1

Consent & language

Pick English, Hindi or Kannada. Optionally upload your bank's own PDF form — we only ask what it needs.

2

Prove it's you

A randomised blink-and-turn liveness challenge, then your live face is matched against your PAN or Aadhaar photo.

3

Just talk

Say “2.5 lakh” or “डेढ़ लाख”. Spoken numbers, dates and PAN letters are understood and validated as you go.

4

Instant, explained decision

Get a score, the top reasons behind it, three offers with real EMIs, and a downloadable decision report.

Questions

Which browsers support voice?

Voice input works in Chrome, Edge and Samsung Internet. In other browsers you can type your answers — everything else works the same.

Is my video uploaded?

No. Liveness and face matching run locally with face-api.js. Only the result (for example “81% match”) is sent with your application.

What if I don't know my CIBIL score?

Just say “skip”. We use a cautious default and a bureau check happens before disbursal.

Is this a real loan?

CredAI is a working demonstration of a digital lending journey. Decisions are indicative and not a binding sanction.
